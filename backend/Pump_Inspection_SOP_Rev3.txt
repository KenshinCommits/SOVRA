STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: CENTRIFUGAL SLURRY PUMP INSPECTION (SOP-PUMP-402)
Document ID: SOP-PUMP-402-REV3
Facility: Industrial Operations Unit 4
Target Equipment: Centrifugal Slurry Pump P-104A / P-104B

1. OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
This procedure specifies mandatory inspection intervals, quantitative thresholds, and corrective protocols for heavy-duty centrifugal slurry pumps in continuous service.

2. INSPECTION CHECKLIST & PARAMETERS
2.1 Vibration Analysis
- Measure RMS velocity in horizontal, vertical, and axial planes at DE and NDE bearing housings.
- Acceptable operating limit: RMS velocity <= 4.5 mm/s.
- Warning alert threshold: 4.6 mm/s to 6.0 mm/s (schedule vibration spectrum analysis).
- Critical trip limit: > 6.0 mm/s (immediate shutdown and lockout).

2.2 Mechanical Seal & Flushing System
- Plan 32 external flush water pressure must be maintained at 15 PSI above stuffing box cavity pressure.
- Maximum allowable seal face leakage: 5 drops per minute.
- Leakage between 6 and 20 drops per minute indicates early seal ring degradation.
- Leakage > 20 drops per minute requires immediate isolation and mechanical seal cartridge overhaul.

2.3 Bearing Assembly & Temperatures
- Normal operating temperature range: 50°C to 72°C.
- Maximum allowable steady-state temperature: 75°C.
- High-temperature alarm threshold: 85°C.
- Safety trip threshold: 95°C.
- Lubrication schedule: Replenish 25g of ISO VG 220 synthetic polyurea grease every 500 operating hours.

2.4 Impeller Clearance & Casing Wear
- Suction liner to impeller front shroud clearance nominal specification: 0.8 mm.
- Maximum permissible clearance before efficiency loss: 1.5 mm.
- Mandatory replacement threshold: clearance exceeding 2.5 mm requires suction liner and impeller changeout.

3. DISPATCH & DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
Any maintenance action, vibration exceedance, or seal cartridge replacement must be formally logged with a generated recommendation report (DOCX) signed off by the Operations Shift Engineer before physical intervention.
